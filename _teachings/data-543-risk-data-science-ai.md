**Role:** Teaching Assistant · Fall 2026 (current)

DATA 543 introduces students to advanced techniques in data science, machine learning, and artificial intelligence, and their application to the management of financial risks. Students learn to discover, process, and visualize natural hazard and financial data, and to quantify risks such as natural hazards and design management strategies to mitigate negative outcomes.

The course teaches basic programming methods alongside data analysis and machine learning techniques for modeling the complex systems that give rise to risk. Structured case studies and in-class assignments build toward longer group projects.

As TA I support students through the modeling assignments and the group project track, where the hard part is usually not fitting a model but deciding what a risk estimate actually licenses you to claim.
