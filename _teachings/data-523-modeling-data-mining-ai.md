**Role:** Teaching Assistant · current semester

DATA 523 explores cutting-edge machine learning methods, their applications across various domains, and foundational methods in modeling and data mining for artificial intelligence. The course covers transfer learning, representation learning, graph mining, generative models, and uncertainty estimation.

Students work through case studies, coding assignments, and group projects addressing real-world AI challenges. As TA I support students on the modeling assignments and the project track, where most of the difficulty tends to be less about the algorithms themselves and more about choosing an evaluation that actually answers the question being asked.
