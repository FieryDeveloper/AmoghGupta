NLP research at **IIT Indore** on extracting actionable information from disaster-related social media during crisis events.

During a disaster, the constraint is not a shortage of information but an unusable excess of it. This project compressed a corpus of **3,000 disaster-related tweets down to the 40 most critical**, using a combination of large language models and **integer linear programming** to make the summarization both accurate and efficient — the ILP formulation lets you optimize coverage under an explicit budget rather than hoping a generative model self-limits.

## Related work

I also contributed to a **flood detection** project in the same group, applying segmentation techniques to satellite and aerial imagery to improve classification outcomes and help identify affected regions.
