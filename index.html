Amazon

May – Aug 2026 Seattle, WA

A3LS: accent regression testing for Alexa

Speech recognition degrades unevenly across accents, and a regression there stays invisible unless you test for it. A3LS generates 5,000+ audio files on the fly across multiple accents for Alexa regression testing, then scores how far recognition drops for each one.

  • A Step Functions pipeline fans synthesis and scoring out across every utterance and accent, reporting per-accent WER and AIER.
  • A React/Cloudscape dashboard over a CloudWatch metrics API surfaces per-accent accuracy, latency percentiles and GPU concurrency.
  • Wrote the HLD/LLD design docs and drove cross-team reviews to add an accent dimension to production ASR test runs.
  • 80+ code reviews across four packages (Smithy, Java, CDK, React), owning CI/CD end to end.

Research

Alignment research at UNC. Safe, without being useless.

I'm a graduate researcher in NLP at UNC Chapel Hill, advised by Prof. Snigdha Chaturvedi. Aligned models often refuse requests they could have answered safely. I work on methods that win that helpfulness back, and I build the training and evaluation pipelines that show whether they do.

EMNLP 2026 Findings

SHARD: Safe and Helpful Alignment via Self-Reframing Distillation

Aligned models over-refuse: they decline a sensitive prompt, or answer with safety boilerplate, when the underlying need could have been met safely. SHARD uses self-reframing distillation to win that helpfulness back without weakening safety.

V. Manoranjan, Amogh Gupta, A. R. Vijjini, T. Hofweber, S. Chaturvedi

arXiv

ACM FAccT 2026

Compounding Disadvantage: Auditing Intersectional Bias in LLM-Generated Explanations Across Indian and American STEM Education

An audit of AI-generated explanations in Indian and American STEM settings. Bias across language, caste and social context does not simply add up. It compounds.

Amogh Gupta, N. R. Patil, S. Ghosh, S. N. S. Gaikwad
